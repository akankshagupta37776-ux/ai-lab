# ai-lab

Welcome to my AI Lab — a growing portfolio of practical work exploring Generative AI, AI systems, automation, data analysis, AI evaluation, prompt engineering, and human-centered problem solving.

This repository documents projects, experiments, tools, and ideas developed while exploring how AI can be turned into useful, reliable, and practical systems.

Experiments with AI-powered workflows, automation systems, and structured problem-solving frameworks.

🧠 Claude Skills

✍️ Prompt Engineering

Structured prompts, prompt systems, and reusable AI workflows designed to improve the quality and consistency of AI outputs.

🔬 AI Evaluation & Reasoning

Experiments exploring how AI responses can be evaluated beyond fluency — including correctness, reasoning quality, assumptions, evidence, and reliability.

📚 Research & Experiments

Notes, prototypes, frameworks, and experiments exploring emerging questions around AI, knowledge, reasoning, and human-AI interaction.
